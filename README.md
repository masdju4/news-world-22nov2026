One image summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 — six panels, one frame.

- **Kyiv hit** — Russian drones and missiles pound the Kyiv region; rescuers dig for survivors near Dymer.
- **COP31 in doubt** — a new draft lands in Antalya, the EU rejects it, and the summit runs past its deadline.
- **Europe marches** — sixth weekend of cost-of-living protests across France, Spain and Portugal.
- **Cartel boss held** — Mexico detains Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa with US help; he topped the FBI's most-wanted list.
- **NVIDIA sell-off** — the AI trade unwinds as investors question the chip giant's accounting.
- **Boat capsizes** — at least 24 die in Nigeria when a boat carrying mourners to a funeral overturns.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`draw_news.py` regenerates the image from scratch (Pillow + stdlib only, offline).
